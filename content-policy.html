Content Policy & Safety Guidelines

Last Updated: January 2024

Zero Tolerance Policy: VComingle has zero tolerance for illegal content, child exploitation, and severe harassment. Violations will result in immediate permanent bans and reporting to authorities when appropriate.

1. Our Commitment

VComingle is committed to providing a safe and respectful environment for random video and text chat. We believe in connecting people worldwide while maintaining high standards of content quality and user safety.

2. Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited on VComingle:

2.1 Sexual Content

  • Nudity or partial nudity
  • Sexually explicit behavior or acts
  • Sexual harassment or solicitation
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) - will be reported to authorities
  • Non-consensual sexual content

2.2 Violence and Harm

  • Graphic violence or gore
  • Self-harm or suicide content
  • Promotion of dangerous activities
  • Threats of violence or harm
  • Stalking or predatory behavior

2.3 Hate Speech and Discrimination

  • Racist, sexist, or discriminatory language
  • Hate speech based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or ethnicity
  • Slurs or derogatory terms
  • Harassment based on protected characteristics
  • White supremacist or extremist content

2.4 Illegal Activities

  • Drug use or drug dealing
  • Illegal weapons or explosives
  • Fraud, scams, or phishing
  • Hacking or cyberattacks
  • Terrorist content or recruitment
  • Any content violating local, state, or federal laws

2.5 Privacy Violations

  • Sharing personal information without consent (doxxing)
  • Recording or capturing others without permission
  • Blackmail or extortion
  • Impersonation of others
  • Sharing private conversations or images

2.6 Spam and Misuse

  • Spam messages or repetitive content
  • Bots or automated accounts
  • Fake accounts or catfishing
  • Scams or fraudulent schemes
  • Malicious links or malware

3. Allowed Content

We encourage: Friendly conversations, cultural exchange, language learning, making new friends, sharing hobbies and interests, and respectful social interaction.

4. User Behavior Guidelines

To maintain a positive experience, we expect users to:

  • Be Respectful: Treat others with kindness and respect
  • Be Honest: Be authentic and don't misrepresent yourself
  • Be Safe: Protect your privacy and don't share personal information
  • Be Patient: Understand that users come from diverse backgrounds
  • Be Responsible: Use the platform appropriately and follow all guidelines

5. Reporting Violations

If you encounter content or behavior that violates our policy:

  • Click the "Report" button during any chat session
  • Provide details about the violation
  • Our team will review reports promptly
  • Repeat offenders will face escalating consequences

6. Enforcement and Consequences

Violations of our Content Policy may result in:

  • Warning: First-time minor violations may receive a warning
  • Temporary Ban: Repeat violations may result in temporary suspension
  • Permanent Ban: Severe or repeated violations result in permanent banning
  • Legal Action: Illegal activities will be reported to law enforcement

7. Age Restrictions

VComingle is strictly for users 18 years and older. We do not allow minors on our platform. If you discover a minor using the service, please report them immediately.

8. Recording and Privacy

  • Do not record other users without their explicit consent
  • Do not share recordings of others without permission
  • Screen capturing of other users is prohibited without consent
  • Violations will result in immediate permanent bans

9. Content Moderation

We employ multiple moderation methods:

  • User reporting system
  • Automated content filtering
  • Human review of reported content
  • Regular policy updates based on user feedback

10. Fair Use and Copyright

Users must respect intellectual property rights:

  • Don't share copyrighted material without permission
  • Don't use others' content without attribution
  • Respect trademarks and brand rights

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Content Policy periodically to reflect changes in our service, user feedback, or legal requirements. We will notify users of significant changes.
