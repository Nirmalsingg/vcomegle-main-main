About VComingle

Connecting people worldwide through random video and text chat since 2024.

Our Mission

To create a safe, fun, and accessible platform where people from all over the world can connect, share experiences, and make new friends through random video and text conversations.

What is VComingle?

VComingle is a modern random video chat platform that connects you with strangers from around the world. Built with WebRTC technology, our platform enables real-time video and text conversations without the need for downloads or registrations.

Our Story

VComingle was founded in 2024 with a simple idea: make random video chat accessible, safe, and enjoyable for everyone. We recognized the need for a platform that balances the excitement of meeting new people with robust safety measures and user-friendly design.

How It Works

VComingle uses WebRTC (Web Real-Time Communication) technology to establish peer-to-peer connections between users. This means:

  • Video and audio streams go directly between users (not through our servers)
  • Lower latency and better quality video calls
  • Enhanced privacy as content is not stored centrally
  • Our signaling server only helps establish the initial connection

Technology Stack

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
  • Real-time Communication: WebRTC, Socket.io
  • Backend: Node.js, Express
  • Hosting: Vercel (frontend), Render (backend)
  • Security: SSL/TLS encryption, Helmet.js security headers

Our Commitment to Safety

We take safety seriously. VComingle includes:

  • Age verification (18+ only)
  • Report button for inappropriate behavior
  • Clear community guidelines and content policy
  • Easy "Next" and "Stop" controls for user comfort
  • No permanent data storage of conversations
